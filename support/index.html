Support

Need help with Fluffy - Escape Puzzle? Email us at weplaystudio1@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the level number if your question is about a level. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

After five failed attempts on a level, its solution becomes available and shows you every move. After ten failed attempts, you can skip the level and come back later. Every level can be solved without buying anything.

A Darkoo caught my Fluffy. What happened?

A Darkoo chases a Fluffy as soon as it has an open path. Close the Darkoos' paths first, then open the way to the exit. Protect, then escape!

I bought Remove Ads or Gems but don't see them.

Open the Shop, go to the Gems & Ads tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same account you used to buy.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Purchases like Remove Ads come back with Restore purchases. Level progress moves only if Android's backup and restore brings the game's data across.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google: Google Play refunds.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings > Apps > Fluffy - Escape Puzzle > Storage > Clear data. We do not keep any player data on servers. See Delete your data.
