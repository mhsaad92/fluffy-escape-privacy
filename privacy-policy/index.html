Privacy Policy: Fluffy - Escape Puzzle

Effective date: 7 November 2026
Developer: WePlayStudio1
Contact: weplaystudio1@gmail.com

Fluffy - Escape Puzzle ("the game") is made by WePlayStudio1 ("we", "us"). The game is designed for everyone, including children. This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, age, photos or any other personal information.
  • There are no user accounts, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are set up for children: no personalised ads, only content rated G, and the game does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: levels completed, stars, coins, gems, awards, daily rewards, settings and the items you have bought. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own device backup. Uninstalling the game deletes it from the device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We do not have servers that receive player data, and the game does not contain analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows banner ads, ads between levels, and optional reward videos that you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because our audience includes children, every ad request from the game is set up for children:

  • ads are never personalised (no interest-based advertising), for any player;
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences (G) are shown;
  • the game does not access the device's advertising ID, and it does not use Android's ad-ID, ad-topics or ad-attribution features.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), the device type and operating system version, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Play Families policies.

A one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") turns off banner ads and ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The game offers optional purchases: Remove Ads and Gem packs. They are made through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we never see or store your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it on your device. Nothing in the game has to be bought to finish a level.

5. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for children and follows the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). The game has no chat, no sharing, no social network links and no way for players to contact each other. Ads are restricted as described in section 3.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child's personal information has reached us, please email weplaystudio1@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Information we share

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases), as described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings. Because we do not keep player data on servers, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. For anything Google processes for ads, see Google's privacy policy and controls at myaccount.google.com.

Step-by-step instructions: Delete your data.

8. Security

Information that Google processes for ads and purchases is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Game progress stays on your device.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings, to delete all game data from the device.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop banner ads and ads between levels.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Since we do not hold personal information, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can always contact us and we will help.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is important, we will also mention it in the game's update notes.
