Fluffy - Escape Puzzle

Slide the blocks. Save every Fluffy.

The Fluffies are lost, and the Darkoos are coming! Slide wooden blocks to build safe paths, shut the Darkoos out and guide every Fluffy home.

Fluffy - Escape Puzzle is a cosy puzzle game with a clever twist: the obvious way out is usually a trap. Look first, close off the danger, and only then open the way.

Highlights

  • 520 puzzles across 13 colourful worlds, from sunny meadows to outer space
  • From gentle first steps to truly tricky Extreme Hard levels
  • Up to four Fluffies to protect at once
  • Every level is tested so it can always be solved, even at a slow, careful pace
  • Helpful items when you want them, never required
  • Made for everyone, including children
