Delete your data: Fluffy - Escape Puzzle

Fluffy - Escape Puzzle is made by WePlayStudio1. The game has no accounts and we do not keep player data on servers. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

Delete the game data on your device

You can delete all game data yourself, at any time:

  • Clear the data: open Android Settings > Apps > Fluffy - Escape Puzzle > Storage > Clear data. This deletes your progress, coins, gems, awards and settings straight away.
  • Or uninstall the game: uninstalling deletes all of its data from the device.

Purchases are recorded by Google Play, not by us. A bought "Remove Ads" can be restored later with Restore purchases in the Shop.

Request deletion by email

If you believe any of your information, or your child's, has reached us, email weplaystudio1@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete it and reply to confirm within 30 days. You do not need an account or any proof of purchase.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: everything the game stores (progress, coins, gems, awards, daily rewards and settings) and any information you send us.
  • Kept by us: nothing. We have no servers that store player data.
  • Handled by Google: limited technical information Google AdMob uses to show child-safe ads (see section 3 of our Privacy Policy). You can manage it with Google at myaccount.google.com.
